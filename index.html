About karmaLINA

Making space for stories, voices and perspectives that matter.

Our Why

karmaLINA Films is a boutique production, film finance, and strategic marketing house working across narrative film, documentary, and emerging formats, with global affiliate offices in Los Angeles, Toronto, Mumbai, Jamaica, and Morocco, and partners across Europe.

Founded by Lina Dhingra following her devastating experience as a victim of an infamous Ponzi scheme, the company grew from a personal desire to tell her story into a broader mission: to champion underrepresented voices, particularly women’s stories, alongside stories of human impact, resilience, and transformation.

With more than twenty years of experience working across Bollywood and Hollywood, Lina has built an extensive network of international filmmakers and creative partners. She is driven to bring powerful stories to audiences beyond their borders—bridging cultures, communities, and perspectives along the way.

karmaLINA seeks out stories with emotional resonance and international appeal, supports meaningful filmmaker partnerships and collaboration, and creates work that invites dialogue across cultures and challenges audiences to see a world beyond their own. At its heart is a simple belief: that stories of love, loss, resilience, enlightenment, and transformation can connect us in ways that transcend borders.
