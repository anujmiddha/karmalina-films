# karmaLINA Films

karmaLINA Films is a boutique production, film finance, and strategic marketing house working across narrative film, documentary, and emerging formats. The studio champions underrepresented voices and stories of human impact, resilience, and transformation.

The website introduces the studio and the people behind its films. Visitors can explore featured productions, read project synopses and team biographies, discover festival recognition, and get in touch with the studio.

Cinematic imagery and video bring the film slate to life, with responsive layouts for desktop, tablet, and mobile, plus light and dark viewing themes.
